Additional privacy notes for online tools

Most Ymir Tool pages are designed to run in the browser for quick local interaction. Even when a page processes text locally, users should treat any online tool as an untrusted place for secrets unless their own security policy explicitly allows that workflow. The safe default is simple: use non-sensitive samples, redact private values, and keep production credentials out of browser utilities.

Examples of content to avoid

Do not paste passwords, API tokens, private keys, signed URLs, customer records, unreleased business data, internal incident logs, authentication cookies, or confidential source code. For these materials, use approved internal tooling that provides access control, audit logs, retention rules, and organizational review.

Advertising and analytics context

Advertising and analytics services may process standard browser information such as page URL, device type, approximate network information, and cookie identifiers according to their own policies. Ymir Tool uses this information to operate the site, understand aggregate usage, and support advertising where allowed. Users can control cookies and tracking behavior through browser settings and privacy tools.

Practical privacy workflow

Before opening a tool, decide whether your input is public, sample, internal, or confidential. Public and synthetic samples are suitable. Internal and confidential content should be redacted or processed in approved environments. After copying a result, verify that no hidden secret, identifier, or customer-specific value remained in the output.
